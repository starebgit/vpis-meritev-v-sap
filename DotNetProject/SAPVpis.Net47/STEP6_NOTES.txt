Confirmed from SAP screenshots
-----------------------------
Function module: BAPI_INSPOPER_RECORDRESULTS
Imports:
- INSPLOT (QALS-PRUEFLOS, NUMC 12)
- INSPOPER (QIBPVORNR / VORNR-based, CHAR 4)
- INSPPOINTDATA (structure present)
- HANDHELD_APPLICATION (present in import list)

Tables:
- CHAR_RESULTS   -> BAPI2045D2
- SAMPLE_RESULTS -> BAPI2045D3
- SINGLE_RESULTS -> BAPI2045D4

BAPIRET2 fields observed:
TYPE, ID, NUMBER, MESSAGE, LOG_NO, LOG_MSG_NO, MESSAGE_V1..V4,
PARAMETER, ROW, FIELD, SYSTEM
